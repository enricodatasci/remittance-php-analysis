# Findings: Sending $200 from the U.S. to the Philippines

*Data: World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide (2011 to Q1 2025, 2,453 price quotes, 25 providers) and ECB daily exchange rates. Last updated October 2026.*

## Summary

Which provider a family uses matters more than almost anything else. In Q1 2025, the cheapest provider delivered **546 more pesos per $200** than the most expensive mainstream option, about **6,550 pesos a year** for a family sending money monthly. Much of the cost is hidden in the exchange rate rather than shown as a fee, and after a decade of falling prices, costs have been rising since 2020.

## 1. Which provider delivers the most pesos?

In Q1 2025, **Wise** ranked first at a **1.19%** total cost, delivering **11,453 pesos** per $200.

| Rank | Provider | Total cost | Pesos received |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wise | 1.19% | 11,453 |
| 2 | M.Lhuillier | 3.00% | 11,243 |
| 3 | Western Union | 3.90% | 11,139 |
| 13 | Johnny Air Cargo | 5.90% | 10,907 |

Wise also finished in the top 3 in **22 of 32 quarters** surveyed, so it's consistently cheap rather than cheap for one quarter.

## 2. Hidden costs

For several providers, most of the cost is the **exchange rate markup**, not the fee:

| Provider | Avg. fee | Avg. FX markup | Share hidden in exchange rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transfast | 0.79% | 1.77% | 69% |
| WorldRemit | 1.91% | 2.33% | 55% |
| Pangea | 2.09% | 2.47% | 54% |

**Lesson for families:** "Low fee" or "no fee" doesn't mean cheap. Compare the **number of pesos that arrive**, not the fee.

## 4. Costs are rising again

The average total cost fell from **6.58% in 2011** to a low of **3.73% in 2020**, then rose to **5.40% in 2024**. Few providers meet the UN Sustainable Development Goal target of **3%** in any year.

## 5. Case study: Citibank's free transfer disappeared

Citibank's account-to-account transfer cost **0% through 2021**, then switched to a **$35 flat fee (17.5% of $200)** starting in 2022. This lines up with Citi's 2022 exit from Philippine consumer banking.

**Lesson for families:** Re-check your provider every year. A flat fee also hurts small transfers most: $35 is 17.5% of $200 but only 7% of $500.

## Recommendations

1. **Compare pesos received, not fees.** Use a comparison tool before sending.
2. **Use a low-cost digital provider** like Wise for routine transfers.
3. **Avoid flat fees on small amounts.** If your provider charges a flat fee, send larger amounts less often.
4. **Re-check providers yearly.** Prices and products change.

## Limitations

- World Bank data reflects **quoted prices** collected each quarter, not actual transactions.
- Provider data ends in **Q1 2025**, and **4 quarters** are missing from the survey.
- **8 quotes** were non-transparent: the provider didn't disclose its exchange rate, so their true cost is likely understated.
- Exchange rates are **quarterly averages**, so movements within a quarter are smoothed out.
- Promotions and first-transfer discounts aren't captured.
